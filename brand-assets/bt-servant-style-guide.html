Identity

Logo

The mark is a speech-bubble “scroll” frame with three typing dots — two neutral, one in Clarity Orange — paired with the “BT Servant” wordmark. Use the full lockup whenever there's room; the mark alone is reserved for small spaces (favicons, avatars, app icons).

Primary lockup — on light backgrounds
Reversed lockup — on dark backgrounds

The mark alone, without the wordmark. Used for favicons, WhatsApp/social avatars, and anywhere the full lockup won't legibly fit. Never pair the icon-only mark with a restatement of the name right next to it — that duplicates the wordmark's job.

Live-text wordmark · Inter · for contexts that can't use the logo image (plain-text email subjects, meta titles, in-app headers, code comments)
BT Servant

BT is always Inter 800 (Extrabold); Servant is always Inter 400 (Regular) — matching the site's own .site-logo__word rule. Never set both words at the same weight.

Do

  • Keep clear space around the mark of at least the height of the “o” dot on every side.
  • Use the light-background lockup on white/paper surfaces, and the reversed lockup on charcoal/navy surfaces.
  • Scale the lockup proportionally; keep the wordmark legible at a minimum width of ~90px.
  • When the wordmark must be set as live text, keep “BT” bold and “Servant” regular — never both the same weight.

Don't

  • Stretch, skew, or squash the lockup.
  • Recolor the mark or dots outside the approved palette.
  • Place the light-background lockup directly on a busy photo or pattern without a solid backing plate.
  • Shrink the full lockup so small the wordmark blurs — drop to the icon mark instead.
  • Set “BT” and “Servant” at the same weight when typing the name out — the imbalance is intentional.

Voice & Tone

BT Servant is a project of unfoldingWord and follows its parent voice — “The Trusted Guide.” BT Servant has no separate documented voice of its own, so this section carries the org-level framework through rather than inventing a new one.

Humble, not heroicElevate local churches and translators; BT Servant is a tool in their hands, not the hero of the story.
Clear, not complicatedPlain language. Define jargon. Match the site's own copy: short sentences, concrete verbs.
Faithful, not flashyNo hype. The copy earns trust with specifics (real resources, real orgs) instead of superlatives.
Hopeful, not naïveHonest about the hard work of translation; grounded in what God is doing through it.
